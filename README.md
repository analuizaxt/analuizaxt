# Ana Luiza Tavares

Master's student in Electrical Engineering at the Federal University of Pará (UFPA), Brazil, in the Applied Computing area, with a research focus on Computational Intelligence.
I hold a B.Sc. in Biotechnology from UFPA and a specialization in Neuroscience and Neurophysiology. My research interests lie at the intersection of **signal processing, computational neuroscience, and machine learning**, with emphasis on linear and nonlinear analysis of electrophysiological recordings for investigating and decoding brain activity.

My background also includes scientific programming, computational modeling, mathematical methods, and the development of machine-learning approaches for biological and biomedical data.

## Research Interests

- Computational Neuroscience
- Electrophysiological Signal Processing
- Neural Dynamics
- Linear and Nonlinear Time-Series Analysis
- Machine Learning
- Computational Modeling
- Biomedical and Biological Data Analysis
